# Cash-flow screen to annual-report reconciliation

The [Screener consolidated cash-flow table](https://www.screener.in/company/540699/consolidated/) is a useful way to spot a question. We then check the exact amounts against Dixon's consolidated cash-flow statement. Figures below are INR crore for years ended 31 March, with negative values shown in parentheses. The site displays rounded whole crores; the annual reports use INR lakh, converted here to crore. This comparison was checked on 8 October 2026.

| Measure | FY2024 | FY2025 | FY2026 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Operating cash flow, annual report | 584.31 | 1,149.75 | 1,782.29 |
| Investing cash flow, annual report | (530.90) | (1,228.90) | (1,250.54) |
| Financing cash flow, annual report | (69.97) | (26.57) | (108.15) |
| Net change from these three activities | (16.56) | (105.72) | 423.60 |
| Cash added through changes in the consolidated group | 0.00 | 136.09 | 112.98 |
| Total change in cash and cash equivalents | (16.56) | 30.37 | 536.58 |
| Screener net cash flow, rounded | (17) | 30 | 537 |

**FY2026 check:** 1,782.29 − 1,250.54 − 108.15 = 423.60. Then 423.60 + 112.98 = 536.58. Opening cash of 230.85 + 536.58 = closing cash of 767.43. FY2025 follows the same pattern: (105.72) + 136.09 = 30.37. FY2024 has no separate group-cash addition in this bridge.

Screener shows FY2026 investing activity of about (1,138) rather than the annual report's (1,250.54). The arithmetic difference is 112.98, exactly the separately disclosed group-cash addition. FY2025 similarly differs by 136.09. **Inference:** the site appears to fold those group-cash additions into its investing row to make its displayed activity rows sum to the total cash movement. This is a presentation reconciliation, not evidence that ordinary investing cash flow improved by that amount. Use the annual report's categories when explaining investing activity.

Screener also shows free cash flow of 16, 254 and 724 for FY2024–FY2026. Our workbook defines *cash after cash capex* as operating cash flow minus the cash-capex outflow identified in the annual reports: (0.12), 210.40 and 714.78. The site's free-cash-flow calculation is not specified in this screenshot and the two series should not be treated as identical. The site reports CFO/OP of 100%, 94% and 117%; our OCF/group PAT ratio uses a different denominator and is 1.56x, 0.93x and 1.08x. Label denominators before comparing ratios.

**Operational implication:** FY2026 operating cash flow increased, but the headline ₹537 crore cash rise contains ₹112.98 crore associated with group changes. A recurring collections improvement cannot be inferred from the closing-cash increase alone. The proposed weekly review should track receivable days, inventory days, supplier terms, disputes and actual borrowing balances. Validate the collections pilot with invoice-level cohorts and financing costs before counting any cash release as a saving.
